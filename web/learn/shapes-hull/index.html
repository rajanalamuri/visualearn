Convex hull

Advanced · ages 13+

Stretch a rubber band around some points. The points it touches make the convex hull. Walk the points from left to right, and whenever a point sticks inwards, drop it.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. sort the points from left to right
  2. build the lower chain: add each point
  3. while the last three turn the wrong way: drop the middle one
  4. build the upper chain the same way, right to left
  5. join the two chains
  • ResultThe outer boundary
  • TimeO(n log n)
  • TestWhich way do three points turn?

Convex hull Advanced

Rubber band

You need A board, 8 pins or coins, and a loop of string or a rubber band · Time 15 minutes

  1. Place 8 pins on a board in a scatter.
  2. Stretch the band around all of them and let it settle.
  3. The pins the band touches are the hull. Write down their order going round.
  4. Try moving one pin inside the band. Does the hull change?

Think about it. Can a point in the middle ever be on the hull?
