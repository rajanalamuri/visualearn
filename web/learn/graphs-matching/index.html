Bipartite matching

Advanced · ages 13+

Pair each person on the left with a different friend on the right, using only the allowed links. If their favourite is taken, ask the one holding it to switch. A chain of switches can make room.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. for each person on the left
  2. for each allowed partner on the right
  3. if the partner is free: pair them
  4. else ask their current partner to try someone else
  5. if that works: pair them
  6. count the pairs
  • FindsThe most pairs
  • MethodTry, and ask to switch
  • TimeO(V·E)

Try it on paper

Bipartite matching Advanced

Dance partners

You need 4 names on the left, 4 on the right, and lines for who may pair · Time 15 minutes

  1. Draw 4 people on the left and 4 on the right. Draw lines for allowed pairs.
  2. Take the left people in order. Pair each with the first allowed partner who is free.
  3. If a left person has only taken partners, ask one of those partners' current pairs to switch to another allowed partner.
  4. Count the pairs. Can you find a way to get more?

Think about it. Why does a chain of switches never leave anyone worse off?
