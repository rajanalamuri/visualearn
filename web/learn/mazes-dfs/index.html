Depth-first search

Starter · ages 8+

Keep a stack of squares to visit, like a pile of plates. Always take the newest one off the top. The search dives down one path as far as it can, then backs up. It finds a route, but not always the shortest.

How it works

  1. stack ← [home]
  2. while the stack is not empty
  3. square ← take from the TOP
  4. if square is school: stop
  5. for each new open neighbour
  6. mark it seen; put on TOP
  7. follow the arrows back home

Try it on paper

Depth-first search Starter

Explore and back up

You need A maze drawn on paper, and a few coins · Time 10 minutes

  1. Draw a maze, or ask someone to draw one for you. Put your finger on the start.
  2. Each time you reach a fork, put a coin on it, then take a way you have not tried.
  3. At a dead end, go back to the last coin you put down and try another way from there.
  4. When every way from a coin has been tried, pick the coin up and go back to the one before.
  5. Keep going until you reach the exit.

Think about it. Your coins are a stack: the last one down is the first one up. Did you find the shortest way out, or just a way?
