Line sweep

Builder · ages 10+

Sweep an invisible line across time and stop only when a meeting starts or ends. Add 1 at a start, take away 1 at an end, and remember the biggest number. That is how many meetings overlap.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. list every start (+1) and end (−1) as an event
  2. sort the events by time (ends before starts at a tie)
  3. sweep through the events
  4. add its +1 or −1 to the running count
  5. remember the biggest count
  • Stops atStarts and ends only
  • TimeO(n log n)
  • AnswerMost at the same time

Sweep the timeline

You need Paper strips marking meetings on a timeline from 0 to 12 · Time 15 minutes

  1. Draw 6 meetings as bars along a timeline.
  2. Write a + at each start and a − at each end, in order along the timeline.
  3. Walk along the line keeping a count: add 1 at a +, subtract 1 at a −.
  4. The biggest count is the number of rooms you need.

Think about it. Why can you ignore all the times when nothing starts or ends?
