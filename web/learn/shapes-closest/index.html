Closest pair of points

Advanced · ages 13+

Which two points are nearest? Checking every pair is slow. Sort the points left to right. For each point, only look at points to its right, and stop when the sideways gap is already bigger than the best distance.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. sort the points by x
  2. best ← the distance of the first pair
  3. for each point i
  4. look at later points j
  5. stop if the x-gap is already at least best
  6. update best if the pair is closer
  • NaiveO(n²)
  • With sortingMuch faster in practice
  • TrickStop when the x-gap is too big

Try it on paper

Closest pair of points Advanced

Nearest neighbours

You need Squared paper with 7 dots · Time 15 minutes

  1. Draw 7 dots on squared paper. Number them left to right.
  2. Start with dot 1 and dot 2 as your best pair. Measure the gap.
  3. For each dot, check the dots to its right. Stop looking as soon as a dot is further away sideways than your best gap.
  4. Mark the closest pair.

Think about it. Why can you stop looking once the sideways gap is as big as the best distance?
