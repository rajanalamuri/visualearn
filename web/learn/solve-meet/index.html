Meet in the middle

Advanced · ages 13+

Trying every subset of 8 numbers means 256 sets. Instead split the numbers in half. List the sums of each half, sort them, and use two pointers to find a pair that adds up to the target.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. split the numbers into a left half and a right half
  2. list every subset sum of each half; sort both lists
  3. left pointer at the smallest left sum, right pointer at the biggest right sum
  4. if the two add to the target: found
  5. if too small move the left pointer up; if too big move the right pointer down

Meet in the middle Advanced

Halve the search

You need Paper and 6 numbers · Time 20 minutes

  1. Write 6 numbers and a target. Split them into two groups of 3.
  2. For each group, list all 8 subset sums (including the empty set, 0). Sort each list.
  3. Put a finger at the start of the left list and another at the end of the right list. Add the two. Too small: move the left finger right. Too big: move the right finger left.
  4. If the fingers add to the target, you have found a subset.

Think about it. For 40 numbers, how many sets would you try one by one? How many sums per half here?
