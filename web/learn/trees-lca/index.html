Lowest common ancestor

Advanced · ages 13+

The lowest common ancestor of two nodes is the first node they both have above them. Lift the deeper node up until both are level, then lift both together until they meet.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. while the two nodes are at different depths
  2. lift the deeper one to its parent
  3. while the two nodes differ
  4. lift both to their parents
  5. where they meet is the answer

Try it on paper

Lowest common ancestor Advanced

Family tree meeting

You need A family tree drawn on paper · Time 10 minutes

  1. Draw a tree of 9 people, parents above children.
  2. Pick two people. Put a finger on each.
  3. Move the finger on the lower person up until both fingers are on the same generation.
  4. Move both fingers up one parent at a time until they land on the same person. That is the lowest common ancestor.

Think about it. Why does lifting the deeper node first make the climb together work?
