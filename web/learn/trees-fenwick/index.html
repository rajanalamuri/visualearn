Fenwick trees

Advanced · ages 13+

A Fenwick tree stores running totals in a clever way. Each cell adds up a block of numbers that ends there. A total of the first few numbers needs only a few cells.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. T[i] = sum of the last (lowest set bit of i) numbers
  2. prefix(k): total ← 0, i ← k
  3. while i > 0:
  4. total ← total + T[i]
  5. i ← i − (lowest set bit of i)
  • Prefix sumO(log n)
  • MemorySame as the row
  • Tricki − (i & −i)

Fenwick trees Advanced

Binary jumps

You need Paper and 8 numbers · Time 15 minutes

  1. Write numbers at positions 1 to 8. Under position 4 write the sum of positions 1 to 4. Under position 6 write the sum of positions 5 and 6.
  2. For position 7 you only need positions 5 to 7... find the pattern: the box under position i covers a block that ends at i, whose length is the biggest power of 2 dividing i.
  3. To add up the first 7 numbers, add the boxes under 7, then 6, then 4.
  4. Check with a slow sum.

Think about it. Why do you jump from 7 to 6 to 4?
