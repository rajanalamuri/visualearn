Two-colouring (bipartite)

Builder · ages 10+

Can you colour the dots with two colours so every line joins different colours? Colour one dot, then give its neighbours the other colour, and keep going. If a line joins two of the same colour, it cannot be done.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. colour the first dot green
  2. take a dot from the queue
  3. each neighbour gets the opposite colour
  4. if a neighbour already has the same colour: impossible
  5. every dot coloured: it works
  • Possible whenNo odd-length loop
  • TimeO(dots + lines)
  • UsesBFS

Try it on paper

Two-colouring (bipartite) Builder

Two crayons

You need A drawing of a loop of dots, and 2 crayons · Time 10 minutes

  1. Draw a loop of 4 dots and join them in a ring. Colour one dot red.
  2. Colour its neighbours blue, then their neighbours red, going round the ring.
  3. Does it work? Now try a loop of 5.

Think about it. Why does an odd-length loop always fail?
