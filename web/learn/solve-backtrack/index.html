Backtracking

Builder · ages 10+

Try a choice. If it leads nowhere, undo it and try another. For each number, take it or leave it. As soon as the total goes over the target, give up on that branch.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. for each number, in order:
  2. try taking it
  3. if the total is over the target: give up (backtrack)
  4. if the total equals the target: found!
  5. then try leaving it out
  6. every choice tried: no answer
  • IdeaTry, undo, try again
  • Saves time byGiving up early
  • Used forPuzzles, mazes, Sudoku

Try it on paper

Backtracking Builder

Pick and undo

You need Paper, a pencil and 5 number cards · Time 15 minutes

  1. Pick a target. Lay out 5 cards. Find cards that add exactly to the target.
  2. Go card by card: take it (add it up) or leave it.
  3. If your total goes over the target, stop and undo the last card you took. Try leaving it out instead.
  4. Write down each take and undo so you can see the search.

Think about it. Why does giving up early on a bad branch save so much work?
