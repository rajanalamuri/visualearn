Insertion sort

Starter · ages 8+

Keep a sorted part on the left. Take the next number and slide it left until it fits, the way most people sort a hand of cards.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. for i = 1 … n−1
  2. key ← a[i]
  3. while left neighbour > key
  4. shift neighbour right; key moves left
  5. key is placed
  • Best caseO(n)
  • Worst caseO(n²)
  • Keeps equal items in order?Yes

Insertion sort Starter

Sort your hand

You need 8 playing cards, face down in a pile · Time 10 minutes

  1. Pick up one card. A hand of one card is already sorted.
  2. Pick up the next card and slide it into the right place in your hand.
  3. Keep going, one card at a time, until the pile is gone.
  4. Now deal the cards out already in order and do it again. Notice how much faster it is.

Think about it. Why does insertion sort get so quick when the cards are nearly in order already?
