Union-find

Advanced · ages 13+

Keep groups as small trees. Each item points to a parent, and the top of a tree is the group's name. To join two groups, hang one top under the other.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. find(x): follow parents until an item is its own parent
  2. point everything on the way straight at the top
  3. union(a, b): find both tops
  4. if they differ, hang one under the other
  5. same top = same group
  • FindFollow parents to the top
  • UnionJoin two tops

Union-find Advanced

Friend circles

You need 8 name cards and some string · Time 15 minutes

  1. Give each person a card and make each their own group (a card pointing at itself).
  2. To join two people: follow arrows from each up to their leader, then make one leader point at the other.
  3. After several joins, ask: are these two in the same group? Follow arrows to the leaders and compare.

Think about it. Why is it worth pointing everyone straight at the leader after looking them up?
