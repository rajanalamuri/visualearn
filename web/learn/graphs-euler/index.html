Euler circuits

Advanced · ages 13+

Can you walk along every road exactly once and end where you started? Yes, if every town has an even number of roads. Walk until you are stuck, then add in any loops you skipped.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. check: every town has an even number of roads
  2. walk along unused roads, noting each town
  3. when stuck, back up and add that town to the finished route
  4. keep going until nothing is left
  5. the finished route (reversed) is the circuit
  • NeedsEvery town has even degree
  • Each roadUsed once
  • MethodHierholzer's walk

Try it on paper

Euler circuits Advanced

Snow plough

You need A drawing of a ring of 6 towns with three extra roads, and a pencil · Time 15 minutes

  1. Draw 6 towns in a ring and add a triangle joining three of them. Count the roads at each town.
  2. Start anywhere and trace every road once without lifting your pencil. If you get stuck, find a town on your route that still has unused roads and walk a loop from there.
  3. You should finish where you began.

Think about it. What goes wrong if two towns have an odd number of roads? What if four do?
