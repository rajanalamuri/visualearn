Interval scheduling

Builder · ages 10+

Many meetings want one room. To fit the most, sort them by when they end. Take the first, then the next one that starts after the last one ended. Finishing early leaves the most room.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. sort meetings by finish time
  2. chosen ← none; last end ← 0
  3. for each meeting in order
  4. if it starts after the last end: choose it
  5. else skip it
  • Sort byFinish time
  • TimeO(n log n)
  • MethodGreedy

Try it on paper

Interval scheduling Builder

Fit the meetings

You need Paper strips of different lengths and a long line marked 1 to 12 · Time 15 minutes

  1. Cut 7 strips and place each along the line where a meeting would run.
  2. Sort the strips by where they end.
  3. Take the first. Then take the next that begins after the last one chosen ends. Skip any that overlap.
  4. Count the meetings you fit.

Think about it. Why sort by the end time and not by the start time or length?
