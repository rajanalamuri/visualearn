Hamilton paths

Advanced · ages 13+

An Euler walk uses every road once. A Hamilton path visits every town once. It sounds similar but is much harder, and nobody knows a quick test. Try a path, and step back when you get stuck.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. start in a town
  2. go to a neighbour you have not visited
  3. if every town is visited: found a Hamilton path
  4. if stuck: step back and try another neighbour
  5. tried every start: there is no Hamilton path
  • VisitsEvery town once
  • Quick test?None known
  • Method hereBacktracking

Try it on paper

Hamilton paths Advanced

Visit every town

You need A drawing of 6 towns joined by roads, and a pencil · Time 15 minutes

  1. Draw 6 towns in a ring and add a couple of extra roads.
  2. Pick a start. Walk to a neighbour you have not visited, marking each town. Try to visit all 6 without repeating one.
  3. If you get stuck, step back one town and try a different road.
  4. Now draw a "star": one town joined to three others, and one more town hanging off two of those. Can you do it?

Think about it. Why can a town with only one road be in the middle of a Hamilton path? (Hint: it cannot.)
