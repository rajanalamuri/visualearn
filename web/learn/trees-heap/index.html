Heaps and priority queues

Builder · ages 10+

A heap keeps the smallest number on top. A new number goes in at the bottom and swaps up while it is smaller than its parent. To take the smallest, remove the top and sink the last number down.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. add: put the new number at the end
  2. while it is smaller than its parent: swap up
  3. remove: take the top
  4. move the last number to the top
  5. while it is bigger than a child: swap with the smaller child
  • SmallestAlways on top
  • Add or removeO(log n)
  • Stored asA plain array

The family pyramid

You need Number cards and a pyramid drawn on paper · Time 15 minutes

  1. Draw a pyramid: 1 box on top, 2 below, 4 below those. Boxes are filled left to right.
  2. Place each new card in the next empty box. If it is smaller than the card above it, swap them, and repeat.
  3. After 7 cards, the smallest is on top. Take it. Put the last card on top and sink it by swapping with its smaller child.
  4. The new top is the second smallest.

Think about it. Where in the pyramid can the third smallest number be?
