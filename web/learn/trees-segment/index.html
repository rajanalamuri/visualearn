Segment trees

Advanced · ages 13+

A segment tree keeps the sum of every chunk of a row: the whole row on top, halves below, single numbers at the bottom. Any stretch can be added up by combining just a few chunks.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. leaves hold the numbers
  2. each parent holds the sum of its two children
  3. query(range): if a chunk is fully inside, use it
  4. if it only partly overlaps, ask both children
  5. if it is outside, skip it
  • BuildO(n)
  • Range queryO(log n)
  • Update one numberO(log n)

Try it on paper

Segment trees Advanced

Sum pyramid

You need Paper and 8 numbers · Time 15 minutes

  1. Write 8 numbers in a row. Above each pair, write their sum. Above each pair of sums, write their sum. The top is the total.
  2. Pick a stretch, like numbers 3 to 7. Find the biggest boxes that fit entirely inside it.
  3. Add just those boxes. Check with a slow sum.

Think about it. How many boxes do you add for the stretch 1 to 7 of 8 numbers?
