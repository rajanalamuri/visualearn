Skip lists

Advanced · ages 13+

A skip list is a sorted list with express lanes. The top lane leaps far. Go right while the next number is not too big, then drop down a lane. It searches fast, like a tree, but it is only lists.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. start at the top lane, left end
  2. if the next number on this lane is not past the target: move right
  3. otherwise: drop down one lane
  4. on the bottom lane: stop at the target, or it is missing
  • SearchAbout log n steps
  • Built fromSorted lanes
  • UsesChance to choose lanes

Try it on paper

Skip lists Advanced

Express lanes

You need Paper: a bottom row of 8 sorted numbers, and shorter rows above · Time 15 minutes

  1. Write 8 sorted numbers on the bottom row. Copy about half of them to a row above, and about half of those to a row above that.
  2. Pick a target. Start at the left of the top row and move right while the next number is not bigger than the target.
  3. Drop down a row and keep moving right. Stop at the bottom row.
  4. Count your moves, and compare with moving along the bottom row only.

Think about it. Why does the top lane help most when the target is far to the right?
