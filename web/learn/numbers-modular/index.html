Modular arithmetic

Starter · ages 8+

Clock arithmetic: numbers wrap around. On a 12-hour clock, 9 o'clock plus 5 hours is 2 o'clock. "Mod 12" means: keep only the remainder after dividing by 12. Computers use it to keep numbers small.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. start at a on a clock with m numbers (0 to m − 1)
  2. move forward b steps, one at a time
  3. past m − 1? wrap round to 0
  4. where you stop is (a + b) mod m
  • Wraps atThe modulus
  • Writtena mod m
  • Used forHashing, clocks, codes

Modular arithmetic Starter

Be a clock

You need A paper clock with the numbers 0 to 6 (or 0 to 11) · Time 10 minutes

  1. Draw a clock with the numbers 0 to 6 round the edge.
  2. Put a coin on 5. Move it forward 4 steps, one number at a time, wrapping from 6 to 0.
  3. Where did it land? Check: (5 + 4) divided by 7, remainder.
  4. Try a few more sums.

Think about it. What is 100 hours after 3 o'clock on a 12-hour clock?
