Randomised algorithms

Builder · ages 10+

Sometimes luck can answer a hard question. Throw darts at a square with a quarter-circle in it. The share that land inside tells you about the circle, and so about the number pi.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. inside ← 0
  2. throw a dart at a random square
  3. if it lands inside the quarter-circle: inside ← inside + 1
  4. after n darts: π ≈ 4 × inside ÷ n
  • UsesRandom choices
  • More dartsBetter answer
  • NameMonte Carlo

Try it on paper

Randomised algorithms Builder

Dart board

You need Squared paper (10 by 10), a dice or coin to choose squares, and a ruler · Time 20 minutes

  1. Draw a 10 by 10 grid and a quarter-circle of radius 10 from the bottom-left corner.
  2. Pick 20 random squares (roll a 10-sided dice twice, or draw numbers from a hat). Mark each one.
  3. Count how many fall inside the curve.
  4. Multiply that count by 4 and divide by 20. How close to 3.14 is it?

Think about it. What happens to your answer if you throw 1000 darts instead of 20?
