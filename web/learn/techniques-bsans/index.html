Binary search on the answer

Advanced · ages 13+

You can binary-search more than a sorted list. If you can test a guess (is this speed fast enough?) and bigger guesses never do worse, halve the range of possible answers.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. low ← 1; high ← biggest possible
  2. while low < high
  3. mid ← the middle guess
  4. if mid works: high ← mid
  5. else: low ← mid + 1
  6. answer is low
  • NeedsA yes/no test
  • TimeO(log range)
  • Instead ofTrying every guess

Binary search on the answer Advanced

Slowest speed that works

You need Paper, a pencil and 4 piles of coins · Time 15 minutes

  1. Make 4 piles of coins. You may eat from one pile per hour, up to your speed. You have H hours.
  2. Guess a speed in the middle. Work out how many hours it takes (each pile needs its size divided by speed, rounded up).
  3. Fast enough? Try a slower speed. Too slow? Try faster. Always guess the middle of what is left.
  4. Stop when only one speed is left.

Think about it. Why can you throw away half the speeds after each test?
