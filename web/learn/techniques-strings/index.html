Strings: palindromes

Starter · ages 8+

A palindrome reads the same backwards. Check it with two pointers: compare the first and last letters, then move inward. One mismatch ends it.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. left ← first; right ← last
  2. while left < right
  3. if letters differ: not a palindrome
  4. left ← left + 1; right ← right − 1
  5. every pair matched: palindrome
  • TimeO(n)
  • PointersBoth ends inward
  • Stops atFirst mismatch

Mirror words

You need Letter tiles or paper slips · Time 10 minutes

  1. Spell RACECAR with tiles in a row.
  2. Point at the first and last tile. Do they match? Move both fingers inward.
  3. Try PENCIL and LEVEL. Stop at the first mismatch.

Think about it. How many comparisons does a word of 100 letters need at most?
