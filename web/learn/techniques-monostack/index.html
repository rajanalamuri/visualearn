Monotonic stacks

Advanced · ages 13+

For each number, find the next bigger number to its right. Keep a stack of numbers still waiting. A new number that is bigger than the top answers it, and lets it leave.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. stack ← empty
  2. for each number n, left to right
  3. while the top is smaller than n:
  4. answer for the top ← n; pop it
  5. push n
  6. whatever is left has no bigger number
  • TimeO(n)
  • Stack holdsNumbers still waiting
  • Instead ofLooking right every time

Monotonic stacks Advanced

Waiting in line

You need A row of 7 number cards and a small pile area · Time 10 minutes

  1. Turn over the cards one at a time from the left. Keep a pile of cards still waiting for something bigger.
  2. When the new card is bigger than the top of the pile, the top has found its answer. Take it off, write the new card next to it.
  3. Keep taking cards off while the new card is bigger. Then put the new card on the pile.
  4. Cards left on the pile never found a bigger one.

Think about it. Each card goes on the pile once and off once. What does that tell you about how much work it is?
