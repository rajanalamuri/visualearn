Greedy choices

Starter · ages 8+

A greedy method always takes the best-looking choice right now and never looks back. Giving change with the biggest coins first works for many coins. But with some coins it gives a worse answer.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. coins taken ← none; left ← the amount
  2. repeat while something is left:
  3. take the biggest coin that fits
  4. subtract it from what is left
  5. count the coins; compare with the best possible
  • IdeaBest choice now
  • SpeedVery fast
  • RiskCan be wrong

Try it on paper

Greedy choices Starter

Change maker

You need Coins or paper discs worth 25, 10, 5 and 1 (or 4, 3 and 1) · Time 10 minutes

  1. Pick an amount, like 63. Always take the biggest coin that still fits.
  2. Count your coins.
  3. Now try coins worth 4, 3 and 1 for the amount 6. Greedy takes 4 + 1 + 1. Can you do it with fewer?

Think about it. For which coins does "biggest first" always give the fewest coins?
