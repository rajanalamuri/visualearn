Counting (nCr)

Builder · ages 10+

How many ways can you choose 2 friends from 5? Pascal's triangle answers it. Each number is the sum of the two above it. The number in row n, place k is "n choose k".

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. row 0 is just 1
  2. each row starts and ends with 1
  3. every other number ← the number above-left + above
  4. n choose k is the number in row n, place k
  • RuleAdd the two above
  • Reads asn choose k
  • EdgesAlways 1

Try it on paper

Counting (nCr) Builder

Build the triangle

You need Paper and a pencil · Time 10 minutes

  1. Write 1 at the top. Below it write 1 1. Then 1 2 1. Each new row starts and ends with 1.
  2. Each number in the middle is the two numbers above it added up.
  3. Go to row 5 and read place 2: that is the number of ways to pick 2 things from 5.
  4. Check by listing the pairs from A, B, C, D, E.

Think about it. Why does choosing 2 from 5 give the same number as choosing 3 from 5?
