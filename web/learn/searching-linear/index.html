Starter · ages 8+

Open the boxes one by one, from the first to the last, until you find the number. Simple, and it works even when the boxes are in no order at all.

Linear search Starter

Find the ace

You need 10 playing cards with one ace, and a friend · Time 10 minutes

  1. Your friend shuffles the cards and lays them face down in a row.
  2. Turn over the cards one at a time, starting from the left.
  3. Make a tally mark for every card you turn. Stop when you find the ace.
  4. Swap roles and play five rounds. Write down how many cards you turned each time.

Think about it. What is the most cards you could ever turn? What is the average? What would it be with a whole deck of 52?
