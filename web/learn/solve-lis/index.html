Longest increasing subsequence

Advanced · ages 13+

Pick numbers from a row, left to right, so each one is bigger than the last. For each number, its best chain is 1 more than the best chain that ends at a smaller number before it.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. best[i] ← 1 for every number
  2. for each number i, left to right
  3. for each earlier number j with a[j] < a[i]
  4. best[i] ← the bigger of best[i] and best[j] + 1
  5. the answer is the biggest best[i]
  • TimeO(n²) here
  • TableOne cell per number
  • MethodDynamic programming

Longest increasing subsequence Advanced

Climbing the row

You need Paper and 8 numbers in a row · Time 10 minutes

  1. Write 8 numbers. Under each, write 1.
  2. Go left to right. For each number look at the numbers before it that are smaller.
  3. Under this number write one more than the biggest number under those smaller ones.
  4. The biggest number written is the length of the longest climb.

Think about it. Why does a number's answer only depend on numbers to its left?
