Game theory (minimax)

Builder · ages 10+

Two players take turns removing 1, 2 or 3 stones. Whoever takes the last stone wins. Work backwards: a pile is a win if one move leaves your opponent a losing pile.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. a pile of 0: the player to move has lost
  2. for each pile size n, smallest first
  3. look at the piles after taking 1, 2 or 3
  4. if any of them is a loss for the opponent: n is a win
  5. else n is a loss
  • PlayersBoth play perfectly
  • WinSome move leaves a loss
  • LossEvery move leaves a win

Game theory (minimax) Builder

Play the stones

You need 13 coins or counters and a friend · Time 15 minutes

  1. Put 13 coins in a pile. Take turns removing 1, 2 or 3. Whoever takes the last coin wins.
  2. Play a few games. Try to find a pile size that is always bad for whoever is about to move.
  3. Work out why multiples of 4 are the losing piles: whatever you take, your friend can take enough to leave another multiple of 4.

Think about it. If you go first with 13 coins, how many should you take?
