Matrices and grids

Builder · ages 10+

A grid is a list of rows. To visit it in a spiral, go right until the edge, then down, left and up. Turn whenever the next square is off the grid or already visited.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. direction ← right
  2. repeat rows × columns times
  3. visit this square
  4. if the next square is off the grid or visited: turn clockwise
  5. step forward
  • Squaresrows × columns

Matrices and grids Builder

Spiral walk

You need Squared paper (3 rows by 4 columns) and a pencil · Time 10 minutes

  1. Draw a grid 4 across and 3 down and write 1 to 12 in order.
  2. Start in the top-left. Go right until you cannot, then down, then left, then up.
  3. Cross out squares as you visit them. Turn whenever the next square is crossed out or is off the grid.
  4. Write the numbers in the order you visited them.

Think about it. How does the spiral know when it has finished?
