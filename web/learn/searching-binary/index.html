Binary search

Starter · ages 8+

When the boxes are in order, open the middle one. If your number is bigger it must be on the right, and if smaller, on the left. Each box you open throws away half of what is left.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. lo ← first box; hi ← last box
  2. while lo ≤ hi
  3. open the middle box, mid
  4. if it is the target: found!
  5. if it is smaller: lo ← mid + 1
  6. else: hi ← mid − 1
  7. the target is not here

Guess my number

You need Two people. Nothing else. · Time 10 minutes

  1. One person thinks of a whole number from 1 to 100 and keeps it secret.
  2. The other person guesses. The answer is only ever "higher", "lower" or "yes!".
  3. Always guess the middle of what is left. Start with 50. If the answer is "higher", the next guess is 75.
  4. Count the guesses. Then swap roles.

Think about it. Seven guesses are always enough for 1 to 100. How many would you need for 1 to 1,000? For every person on Earth?
