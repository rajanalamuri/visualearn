Mo's algorithm

Advanced · ages 13+

You have lots of questions about stretches of a row. Keep one window and slide its ends instead of starting over each time. Answer the questions in a smart order so the window barely moves.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. sort the questions by (block of the left end, right end)
  2. for each question in that order
  3. move the left end to the question's left, adding or removing one number at a time
  4. move the right end the same way
  5. the window now holds exactly the question's stretch: read the answer
  • WindowSlides, never restarts
  • OrderBy block, then right end
  • Total movesAbout n√q

Mo's algorithm Advanced

Sliding frame

You need A strip of 9 numbers from 1 to 4, and a card with a window · Time 20 minutes

  1. Write 9 numbers from 1 to 4. Make a tally of which numbers are in the window.
  2. Take 4 questions of the form "how many different numbers are between place l and place r?".
  3. Instead of re-counting each from scratch, slide the window ends from the last question to the next, adding or removing one number each step and updating the tally.
  4. Count your steps and compare with counting every stretch from scratch.

Think about it. Why does sorting the questions make the window move less overall?
