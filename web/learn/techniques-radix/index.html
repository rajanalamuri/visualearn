Radix sort

Advanced · ages 13+

Sort by the last digit first, using ten buckets and keeping the order. Then sort by the tens digit the same way. After the last digit, the whole row is sorted.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. for each digit place, last digit first:
  2. put each number in the bucket for its digit
  3. collect the buckets in order, 0 to 9
  4. sorted after the last place
  • TimeO(n × digits)
  • Compares?Never
  • KeyKeep the order inside buckets

Radix sort Advanced

Bucket shuffle

You need Ten cups labelled 0 to 9 and 8 two-digit number cards · Time 15 minutes

  1. Put each card in the cup that matches its last digit. Add cards on top, so the order inside a cup is kept.
  2. Collect the cards cup by cup, from 0 to 9, keeping each cup in order.
  3. Now put each card in the cup for its first digit, and collect again.
  4. The cards are in order.

Think about it. Why must the second round keep the order from the first round inside each cup?
