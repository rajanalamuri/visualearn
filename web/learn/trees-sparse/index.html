Sparse tables

Advanced · ages 13+

A sparse table writes down the smallest number in every block that is 1, 2, 4 or 8 long. Any stretch is covered by two blocks that overlap, so each question takes one look.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. level 0: the numbers themselves
  2. level k: min of two blocks of level k−1
  3. query(l, r): k ← biggest power of 2 that fits
  4. answer ← min(block at l, block ending at r)
  • BuildO(n log n)
  • QueryO(1)
  • Works forMin, max, gcd

Try it on paper

Sparse tables Advanced

Overlapping blocks

You need Paper and 8 numbers · Time 15 minutes

  1. Write 8 numbers. Underneath, write the smaller of each neighbouring pair. Underneath that, the smallest of each 4 in a row.
  2. To find the smallest in a stretch of 6, pick the biggest block that fits (4). Use one block at the start and one ending at the end. They overlap.
  3. The smaller of those two block values is the answer.

Think about it. Why does the overlap not matter for "smallest"? Would it matter for "sum"?
