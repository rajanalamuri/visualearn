Chinese remainder theorem

Advanced · ages 13+

Say a number leaves remainder 2 when divided by 3, and remainder 3 when divided by 5. Mark the numbers that fit the first clue, then the second. The number in both lists is the answer.

Play this lesson

How it works

  1. list the numbers 0 up to the product of the moduli − 1
  2. mark those with the first remainder
  3. mark those with the second remainder
  4. the number marked twice is the answer
  • NeedsModuli with no common factor
  • Unique belowTheir product
  • Used forBig-number tricks, codes

Try it on paper

Chinese remainder theorem Advanced

Two clues

You need A number line from 0 to 14 on paper, and two coloured pencils · Time 10 minutes

  1. Draw the numbers 0 to 14.
  2. Clue 1: the number leaves remainder 2 when divided by 3. Colour all numbers that do (2, 5, 8, 11, 14).
  3. Clue 2: remainder 3 when divided by 5. Colour with the other pencil (3, 8, 13).
  4. The number with both colours is the answer.

Think about it. Why do the clues pick out exactly one number below 15?
