For teachers

Every lesson fits a 40-minute class with one screen at the front of the room.

  1. 10 minutes, together on screen. Turn on Predict mode and let the class vote on each answer before you click.
  2. 25 minutes, in groups, off screen. Print the activity card. Each group needs only cards, coins or paper.
  3. 5 minutes, back together. Compare tally counts with the computer's. Discuss the "Think about it" question.

For parents and grown-ups

Children growing up with AI will get answers instantly. What they need is the habit of thinking a problem through step by step, guessing, and checking. VisuaLearn uses the screen for the minimum it takes to show an idea, then sends them off it.

  • Free for everyone. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
  • No streaks, badges, points or notifications built to pull them back.
  • No autoplay: a lesson never rolls straight into the next one.
  • Every lesson ends with something to do away from the screen.
  • A daily time limit, counted on this device only.

The limit covers every lesson. When time is up, the lesson pauses and suggests the offline activity. Children can add five minutes twice a day. The setting is saved in this browser and nowhere else.
