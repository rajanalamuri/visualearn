Privacy

VisuaLearn is made for children. It collects nothing about them.

Children

  • No accounts, names, emails or photos.
  • No ads. No analytics. No tracking of any kind.
  • No requests to other sites. Fonts and code are served from this site.
  • The diary and the daily time limit are saved on the device only (browser storage). Clearing site data removes them.
  • Read aloud uses the device's own voice. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Parents (optional)

  • A grown-up can sign in with Google on the parents page to keep the diary in step across devices.
  • We store the diary (which ideas, on which days) under the parent's account id. Nothing else, and no child details.
  • Delete the cloud copy at any time from the parents page. Signing out leaves the on-device diary untouched.
  • Google handles the sign-in. See Google's own privacy policy for how it treats your account.
